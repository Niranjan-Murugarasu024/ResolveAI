# Subscription Management & Upgrades

## 1. Subscription Tiers
1. **Free / Developer:**
   - 1 seat, 60 RPM, 1,000 monthly tickets, community support.
2. **Pro ($99/month):**
   - 5 seats, 600 RPM, 50,000 monthly tickets, 99.9% SLA, email/chat support.
3. **Enterprise (Custom pricing):**
   - Unlimited seats, 6,000+ RPM, custom SLA, dedicated TAM, VPC peering, SSO/SAML.

---

## 2. Upgrades & Proration
- Upgrading from Free to Pro is instantaneous upon payment authorization.
- Mid-cycle upgrades are charged on a strictly prorated basis for the remaining days in the billing period.
- Enterprise upgrades require contracting and account manager assignment.

---

## 3. Cancellations & Downgrades
- Customers can cancel or downgrade at any time via Account Settings -> Subscriptions.
- Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing cycle.
- Existing API keys remain valid until the period expires, after which tier limits are reduced to Free limits.
